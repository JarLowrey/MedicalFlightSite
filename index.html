Before your appointment

Pre-flight your medical exam.

Complete MedXpress before your appointment and bring the information your AME will need to review.

1

Complete MedXpress

Fill out your FAA application before the appointment, including medical history information from throughout your life.

2

Save your number

Write down or print your MedXpress confirmation number. You will need it at your AME appointment.

3

Bring documentation

Bring valid photo ID, glasses or contacts if used, and any FAA-requested medical documentation.

4

Review together

Your application is reviewed with you at the appointment before it is submitted to the FAA.

What happens next

Know what to expect.

After your appointment, a certificate is issued if requirements are met. Some applications may be deferred to the FAA for follow-up.

If more information is needed

If the AME requests additional records before issuing your certificate, the document says you have one week to retrieve and submit the requested information. After that, the application must be submitted to the FAA for its judgment and timeline.
